Sources, freshness & coverage

Nova Scotia covers self-serve regular and diesel in all six pricing zones. PEI includes self-serve and full-serve regular, mid-grade, premium and diesel, plus the after-tax maximum for furnace oil. Other provinces and propane are not included.

Sources are checked twice daily. PEI normally adjusts gasoline, diesel and furnace oil at 12:01 a.m. Tuesday and Friday. Extraordinary adjustments can occur. “Effective” is the publisher's edition; “checked” is the last successful retrieval. A source outage keeps the previous good capture and displays a warning.

Changes are either explicitly published by IRAC (self-serve minimum or oil maximum, labelled) or differences from an earlier collected edition. No missing history is invented. NS historical prices · PEI current prices and orders.
